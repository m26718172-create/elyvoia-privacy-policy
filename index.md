# ELYVOIA Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 October 2026**

## 1. Introduction

ELYVOIA is a household assistant app designed to help users remember household items, manage food inventory, organise household information and discover recipes.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use ELYVOIA.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, ELYVOIA may process:

- **Household information:** Item names, descriptions, appliance details, warranty information, receipts and maintenance records you choose to enter.
- **Food inventory information:** Food names, quantities, storage locations, scan history and corrections you provide.
- **Photos:** Images you choose to capture or select for scanning household items or food.
- **Information you provide to AI features:** Questions, prompts and information submitted when using the assistant or recipe features.
- **Subscription information:** Subscription status and purchase-related information needed to provide paid features.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information unless a feature specifically requires it.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and improve ELYVOIA's features, including household memory, inventory management, food scanning, recipe suggestions, shopping lists and subscription access.

Information may also be used to troubleshoot problems, maintain app functionality and respond to support requests.

## 4. Storage and Processing

Some information may be stored locally on your device or processed by services used to operate ELYVOIA.

The precise storage, retention and deletion arrangements depend on the features and services implemented in the app. Users should not assume that deleting the app automatically deletes every copy of their information.

## 5. Third-Party Services

ELYVOIA may use third-party services for app distribution, subscriptions, analytics, data storage or AI-powered features.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

The specific providers and information shared with them should be confirmed against the services currently enabled in ELYVOIA.

## 6. Purchases and Subscriptions

ELYVOIA subscriptions are offered through Google Play. RevenueCat may be used to manage subscription status and entitlements.

Payment processing is handled through the applicable app store. ELYVOIA does not intend to collect or store your full payment card details.

Google Play's own terms and privacy policy also apply to purchases made through Google Play.

## 7. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information processed by ELYVOIA. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should keep their devices and accounts secure.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

To ask about accessing, correcting or deleting information associated with ELYVOIA, contact the app developer using the contact details provided in the app's store listing.

The available deletion process and any exceptions should be confirmed before relying on this policy.

## 9. Children's Privacy

ELYVOIA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the app developer.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information, or to raise concerns about how it is handled.

Australian users may have rights under applicable Australian privacy laws. Requests can be made using the developer contact details provided in the app's store listing.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ELYVOIA's features, services or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised update date where appropriate.

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the ELYVOIA developer using the contact details provided in the ELYVOIA Google Play Store listing.
