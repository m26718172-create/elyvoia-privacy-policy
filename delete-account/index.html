Request account deletion

Your household, remembered. You can request deletion of your ELYVOIA account and associated household data even if you have uninstalled the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Use the button below to email the ELYVOIA support inbox.
  2. Send the request from the email address associated with your ELYVOIA account, if possible. Put Delete my ELYVOIA account in the subject line.
  3. We may contact you to verify account ownership before carrying out the request. Never email your password, payment-card details or verification codes.
Email ELYVOIA to request deletion

If the email button does not work, send your request to elyvoia.test@gmail.com.

What the request covers

An account-deletion request covers your ELYVOIA account and associated data held by ELYVOIA, including, where applicable:

  • Account and sign-in records.
  • Cloud household memory and backup records linked to the account.
  • Other account-linked records managed by ELYVOIA, such as saved warranties or AI feature information where stored.

Data saved only on your device may also need to be removed from that device. We will explain any additional steps needed.

What may be retained

Information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention or accounting requirements may be kept only for the applicable purpose and period. We will explain any applicable exceptions when responding to your request. Google Play purchase and billing records may also be subject to Google's own retention practices.

Have an active subscription? Deleting your ELYVOIA account does not necessarily cancel a Google Play subscription. To avoid future charges, cancel your subscription separately in Google Play subscriptions.

Privacy policy
