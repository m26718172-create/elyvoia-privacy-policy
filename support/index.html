Customer Support & Help Centre

Welcome to ELYVOIA Support

Need help with ELYVOIA? Find answers to common questions or contact our support team.

Account & Sign In

Open ELYVOIA and select Create Account to register, or Sign In if you already have an account.

If your account requires email confirmation, check your inbox and spam folder.

If you cannot access your account, contact support using your registered email address. Never email your password.

Basic & Premium Subscriptions

ELYVOIA offers subscription plans with features and pricing shown inside the app.

On iPhone, subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account.

To manage an Apple subscription, open: iPhone Settings > Your Name > Subscriptions.

On Android, manage subscriptions through Google Play.

Restore Purchases

If you have an active subscription but cannot access your paid features, open ELYVOIA's subscription screen and select Restore Purchases.

Use the same Apple Account or Google Play account associated with the original purchase.

If the problem continues, contact support.

Food Scanner & Recipes

ELYVOIA helps you organise food information and explore meal ideas.

Check that camera permissions are enabled if the scanner cannot access your camera.

AI-generated information may be inaccurate. Always check food safety, allergens and recipe information yourself.

Household Items, Warranties & Maintenance

Use ELYVOIA to organise household belongings, record item details and manage available warranty and maintenance information.

If an item or document is missing, check that you are signed into the correct account.

Shopping Lists & Household Tasks

Create shopping lists and organise household tasks from within ELYVOIA.

If information does not appear as expected, check your internet connection and reopen the app before contacting support.

Ask Elvia — AI Household Assistant

Ask Elvia can help with household questions, organisation and everyday planning.

AI responses may contain mistakes. Verify important information before relying on it for purchasing, health, safety or maintenance decisions.

Privacy & Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your account, please review our account deletion instructions.
